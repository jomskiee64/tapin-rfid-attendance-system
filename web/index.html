From card to confidence.

TAPIN connects the physical entrance with the digital record in three quiet, reliable steps.

01

Tap the card

An RFID reader captures the card UID and sends the event securely to the TAPIN API.

02

Match the person

The system finds the registered user, records the timestamp, and identifies the device.

03

See the day

Authorized staff see current activity, device health, and attendance context in one dashboard.
